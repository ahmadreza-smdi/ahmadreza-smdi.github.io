### 100-word biography

Ahmadreza Samadi is a technology entrepreneur based in Dubai. He builds AI products as Co-Founder & Technical Lead of Appraiva and runs Royal Abraj Group as Co-Founder & CEO. Appraiva is based in Cleveland, Ohio, in the United States. Samadi works remotely from Dubai, connecting property imagery, records and search. At Royal Abraj Group, he leads digital strategy for property brokerage and documents clearing. His work includes websites, CRM workflows and a customer portal. His background includes data engineering at Oxfam Novib and machine learning at MCI. His public guides cover human review and the cost per accepted AI result.

### Background

Ahmadreza Samadi’s background spans enterprise data engineering and machine learning. At Oxfam Novib, he built Azure data infrastructure for CRM migration and analytics, using Databricks, Data Factory, SQL, Key Vault and Azure DevOps. The work included Bronze, Silver and Gold data layers, anonymization and retention logic. At MCI, he developed customer segmentation models using RFM analysis and behavioral data, with work on churn-risk analysis and campaign targeting. Samadi holds a Master of Science in Computer Software Engineering, specialization in Data, from Shahid Beheshti University, 2019–2021. At Appraiva, his responsibilities connect property imagery analysis, property matching, enrichment, search, AI and cloud architecture. At Royal Abraj Group, his system responsibilities cover websites, CRM workflows, a customer portal and internal tools. His public work includes a 393-metro rent and ownership study, a 386-area rent-burden study and calculators for rental cash flow and Dubai purchase costs.

ACS 2024 rent burden across 386 US metros

AI-assisted, reproducible analysis of renter-occupied housing units with computable rent-to-income ratios. Units without a computable ratio are excluded from the percentage denominator and reported separately. Includes methods, data and citation metadata; not property yield or an investment ranking. Resources are in English.
