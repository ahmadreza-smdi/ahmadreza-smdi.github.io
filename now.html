Public tools and data

On 18 October I published Evidence Note, a browser-local editor for recording a claim, its source and the next check. It exports HTML, Markdown and JSON, and can reopen a saved JSON note. Source relationships are recorded by the user and are not independently verified by the editor. Its source, download and project page are public on GitHub and Peerlist.

My recent public work includes an AI workflow cost ledger with fictional worked examples, an ACS 2024 rent-burden study covering 386 US metropolitan areas, and a video publication-date checker. Code, data and methods are linked from my media resources.

These releases were prepared with AI assistance. The examples are teaching material and the study is descriptive; they do not report company results, establish investment returns or independently verify the quality of an AI answer.

I am preparing a comparison of house price growth and a rent benchmark. The published methods now specify a five-year primary window policy; exact dates and results remain unresolved.
