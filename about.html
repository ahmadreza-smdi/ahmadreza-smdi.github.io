About Ahmadreza Samadi

Ahmadreza Samadi · Ahmad Samadi · احمدرضا صمدی

Ahmadreza Samadi is a Dubai-based technology entrepreneur, Co-Founder & Technical Lead of Appraiva and Co-Founder & CEO of Royal Abraj Group. He builds AI for better property decisions.

Continue reading: AI for property decisions · Signature ideas

I’m Ahmadreza Samadi, a technology entrepreneur based in Dubai. I build AI products at Appraiva and lead digital strategy at Royal Abraj Group. My work brings together product decisions, engineering and the practical demands of running a business.

I’m the Co-Founder & Technical Lead at Appraiva, a US-based company in Cleveland, Ohio, where I work remotely and lead the technical foundation of an AI-driven real estate intelligence platform. My responsibilities span product architecture, data systems, AI workflows, and cloud infrastructure, alongside customer discovery and go-to-market strategy. The platform helps investors and developers discover and assess property opportunities through imagery analysis and enriched property data.

I’m also the Co-Founder & CEO of Royal Abraj Group, a Dubai-based business spanning property brokerage and documents-clearing services. I lead digital strategy and technology across the group’s websites, CRM workflows, customer portal, and internal team tools, connecting business priorities with the client experience and day-to-day operations.

Before Appraiva, I worked across enterprise data engineering, machine learning, software development, and business consulting. At Oxfam Novib, I built Azure-based data infrastructure supporting CRM migration and analytics. At MCI, I developed predictive customer segmentation systems. My work in education technology and real estate consulting extended that experience into product infrastructure and customer-facing workflows.

Those experiences shape how I approach entrepreneurship. I start with the customer’s problem, identify where technology can make a useful difference, and connect the product decisions with the engineering required to deliver it.

I’m interested in building technology businesses, developing practical applications of AI, and working with people who bring complementary industry knowledge, commercial experience, and technical expertise.
