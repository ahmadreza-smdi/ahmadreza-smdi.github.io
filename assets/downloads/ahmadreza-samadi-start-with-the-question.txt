START WITH THE QUESTION
A short reading guide to Ahmadreza Samadi's public resources

Choose the question you need to answer. Read the linked resource's source dates,
method and limits before using its output.

1. What does a caller-accepted AI result cost?
Open: AI workflow cost ledger, v0.1.0
https://github.com/ahmadreza-smdi/ai-workflow-cost/releases/tag/v0.1.0
Use it to include failed attempts and retries in cost per distinct accepted result.
Limit: the caller supplies acceptance; the tool does not independently verify
quality, productivity or return on investment.

2. What should count as success before an AI experiment?
Open: Decision-first AI editable text kit
https://github.com/ahmadreza-smdi/ahmadreza-smdi/tree/5d2637b75c672c3fe0596c3d79902de0f77fae63/docs/templates/decision-first-ai
Use the blank planning card and clearly fictional example to make evidence and
acceptance rules explicit before an experiment.
Limit: this is a planning aid, not a validated assessment or evidence that an
experiment was run.

3. How common is rent burden in a US metro?
Open: ACS 2024 metro rent-burden study, v1.0.0
https://github.com/ahmadreza-smdi/metro-rent-burden/releases/tag/v1.0.0
Use its CSV, chart and method to inspect rent-burden shares.
Limit: the denominator is renter-occupied housing units with a computable ratio;
not-computed units are excluded. The 50%+ group is included in the 30%+ group.
These are ACS 2024 statistics, not current asking rents or an investment ranking.

4. Which assumptions drive a rent-and-ownership cash-budget scenario?
Open: Rent and ownership study, v1.0.0
https://github.com/ahmadreza-smdi/rent-own-metro-study/releases/tag/v1.0.0
Use the model and documented source vintages to inspect hypothetical cash budgets.
Limit: HUD gross two-bedroom rents and ACS owner-stock values describe different
housing populations. Financing, operating costs and utility allowances are chosen
assumptions; cash surplus is not accounting profit, property yield or total return.

5. Is FHFA metro data available at both endpoints?
Open: FHFA paired endpoint coverage companion
https://github.com/ahmadreza-smdi/ahmadreza-smdi/tree/fc550a76f8fa08c480c8047224973d34d449701f/docs/research/fhfa-coverage
Use the frozen source snapshots and local runner to reproduce paired availability.
Limit: availability is not price growth, a selected study window or a match to HUD
rent geographies. This companion is not the completed price-versus-rent study.

6. Are my local video publication dates internally consistent?
Open: Video publication-date linter documentation, pinned revision
https://github.com/ahmadreza-smdi/video-date-lint/blob/9dbd8cc1db1db2199487c9a448a63645a63c58b9/README.md
Use the offline CLI to inspect local video JSON-LD and optional sitemap dates
without modifying the input files.
Limit: its local date policy is not a universal search-engine rule or proof of
actual publication, crawling or indexing. This pinned documentation is historical;
read the repository's current documentation before using a newer version.

7. Which Dubai property source answers this question?
Open: Which Dubai property source answers your question?
https://a-samadi.com/writing/which-dubai-property-source.html
Use the guide to distinguish a sales-price index, a rental benchmark and a
separate property-valuation service before interpreting a number.
Limit: the guide retains its historical methodology and reference periods. It is
educational analysis, not a fresh market report, an appraisal or a legal ruling.
