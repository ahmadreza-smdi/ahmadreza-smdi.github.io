EXTENDED BIOGRAPHY

Ahmadreza Samadi is a technology entrepreneur based in Dubai, the Co-Founder & Technical Lead at Appraiva, and the Co-Founder & CEO of Royal Abraj Group.

At Appraiva, he is building AI real estate intelligence tools for investors and developers. At Royal Abraj Group, he leads digital strategy and technology for a Dubai-based business spanning property brokerage and documents-clearing services.

Before Appraiva, Ahmadreza worked in data engineering at Oxfam Novib, machine learning at MCI, and product and data development in education technology. He also advised Bayshire Realty on digital strategy and AI-enabled operations.
