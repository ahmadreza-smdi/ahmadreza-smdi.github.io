EXTENDED BIOGRAPHY

Dubai-based technology entrepreneur Ahmadreza Samadi is Co-Founder & Technical Lead of Appraiva, building AI real estate intelligence, and Co-Founder & CEO of Royal Abraj Group. His work starts with the decision, then the AI. He publishes open property-data studies, practical decision tools and plain-language technology explainers at his website, a-samadi.com. Appraiva is based in Cleveland, Ohio; he works remotely from Dubai. Its AI helps investors find distressed and off-market properties through imagery analysis and enriched property data. Royal Abraj Group spans property brokerage and documents clearing. His background includes enterprise data engineering and machine learning at Oxfam Novib and MCI.
