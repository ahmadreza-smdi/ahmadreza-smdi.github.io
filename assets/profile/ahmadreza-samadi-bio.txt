EXTENDED BIOGRAPHY

Ahmadreza Samadi builds AI products at Appraiva and runs Royal Abraj Group in Dubai. He writes about how businesses decide what to build, check and measure. As Appraiva’s Co-Founder & Technical Lead, he works remotely from Dubai with the Cleveland, Ohio company, connecting property imagery, records and search. As Co-Founder & CEO of Royal Abraj Group, he leads digital strategy for property brokerage and documents clearing, including websites, CRM workflows and a customer portal. His background includes data engineering at Oxfam Novib and machine learning at MCI. His public guides cover human review and the cost per accepted AI result.
