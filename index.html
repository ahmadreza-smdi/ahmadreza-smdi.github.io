About

Technology, product, and business.

I’m Ahmadreza Samadi, a technology entrepreneur based in Dubai. My work brings together product strategy, engineering, and business execution, from understanding a market problem to building the systems that address it.

I’m the Co-Founder & Technical Lead at Appraiva, where I lead the technical foundation of an AI-driven real estate intelligence platform. My responsibilities span product architecture, data systems, AI workflows, and cloud infrastructure, alongside customer discovery and go-to-market strategy. The platform helps investors and developers discover and assess property opportunities through imagery analysis and enriched property data.

I’m also the Co-Founder & CEO of Royal Abraj Group, a Dubai-based business spanning property brokerage and documents-clearing services. I lead digital strategy and technology across the group’s websites, CRM workflows, customer portal, and internal team tools, connecting business priorities with the client experience and day-to-day operations.
