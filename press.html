100-word biography

Ahmadreza Samadi is a Dubai-based technology entrepreneur, Co-Founder & Technical Lead of Appraiva, which builds AI real estate intelligence, and Co-Founder & CEO of Royal Abraj Group. Appraiva is based in Cleveland, Ohio, United States; he works remotely from Dubai. Its tools help real estate investors and developers find distressed and off-market properties through imagery analysis and enriched property data. At Royal Abraj Group in Dubai, he leads digital strategy and technology across property brokerage and documents-clearing services. His background spans enterprise data engineering and machine learning, including work at Oxfam Novib and MCI. He studied at Shahid Beheshti University.
