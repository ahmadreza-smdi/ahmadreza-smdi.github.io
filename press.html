Extended biography

Ahmadreza Samadi is a technology entrepreneur based in Dubai, the Co-Founder & Technical Lead at Appraiva, and the Co-Founder & CEO of Royal Abraj Group.

At Appraiva, a US-based company in Cleveland, Ohio, he works remotely, building AI tools that help real estate investors and developers find distressed and off-market properties using imagery analysis and enriched property data. At Royal Abraj Group, he leads digital strategy across websites, CRM workflows, customer tools, and internal operations for a Dubai-based business spanning property brokerage and documents-clearing services.

His work spans product architecture, AI workflows, data systems, cloud engineering, customer discovery, and go-to-market strategy. He is interested in how technology becomes a useful business: understanding a problem, developing a product people can use, and making the decisions needed to deliver it reliably.

Before Appraiva, Ahmadreza worked in data engineering at Oxfam Novib, machine learning at MCI, and product and data development in education technology. He also advised Bayshire Realty on digital strategy and AI-enabled operations.
