How is the comparison calculated?

The median is the middle value after sorting; for an even count it is the average of the two middle values. Spread is (highest − lowest) ÷ median × 100. Each estimate is flagged only when its absolute difference from the median is more than 10%. Display rounding does not change the underlying flag.

The 10% threshold is this tool’s transparent screening convention, not a valuation standard, confidence interval or demonstrated error rate. Two estimates can have a spread above 10% while neither is more than 10% from their median. Three estimates copied from the same database are not three independent checks.

A narrow spread can still describe the wrong property or repeat an old observation. A wide spread can reflect different dates, condition assumptions or purposes.
