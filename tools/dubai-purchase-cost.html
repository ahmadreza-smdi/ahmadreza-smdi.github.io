    Buyer-paid fee breakdown

    Official fee basis

    The statutory sale fee is 4%, shared equally unless agreed otherwise. The mortgage percentage is 0.25% of registered debt. Partner and document charges depend on the service and quote; a combined sale/mortgage transaction may share fixed charges.

    Relevant FTA FAQs: Residential property treatment and the standard VAT rate.

    Scope and limits

    Existing residential property is generally VAT-exempt; service-fee VAT is a separate matter. This tool adds 5% only to the published sale partner service fee. Your other quotes must already include any VAT. No blanket VAT is added to the residential purchase price.

    The mortgage service page lists an ordinary partner service at AED 4,000 plus VAT and repeats a title-deed charge. This calculator requires your combined quote instead of stacking another fixed set. No provisional Oqood fee is preset.

    Sources were checked on 11 October 2026. The DLD site's 7 October footer is a site-wide update date, not a fee-effective date. Recheck the current rules and exact channel before using a total.
