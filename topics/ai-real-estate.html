AI in real estate: evidence and decisions

Follow a property signal through the questions that make it useful: what supports it, who checks it, and what decision comes next.

By Ahmadreza Samadi

From an image to a research workflow

At Appraiva, my work connects property imagery analysis with property identity, data enrichment and search. I work remotely from Dubai as Co-Founder & Technical Lead of the company based in Cleveland, Ohio, United States.

The case study explains those connections. The readings below then examine four checks around a property decision: the data behind an estimate, human review, the cost of useful AI work, and the difference between rental yield and cash flow.

Keep these questions in view

  • Which property and source records support the result?
  • Who checks the important claims before someone acts?
  • What does the full workflow cost, and what money remains after property costs and loan payments?
