AI in real estate: evidence and decisions

Follow a property signal through the questions that make it useful: what supports it, who checks it, and what decision comes next.

By Ahmadreza Samadi

From an image to a research workflow

Co-Founder & Technical Lead, Appraiva. Appraiva is a US company in Cleveland, Ohio; he works remotely from Dubai. It builds AI tools that help real estate investors and developers find distressed and off-market properties through imagery analysis and enriched property data.

The case study explains those connections. The readings below then examine five checks around a property decision: matching an image to property identity and dated sources, the data behind an estimate, human review, the cost of useful AI work, and the difference between rental yield and cash flow.

Read the Appraiva case study: AI tools for real estate investors and developers, using imagery analysis and enriched property data.

Keep these questions in view

  • Which property and source records support the result?
  • Who checks the important claims before someone acts?
  • What does the full workflow cost, and what money remains after property costs and loan payments?
