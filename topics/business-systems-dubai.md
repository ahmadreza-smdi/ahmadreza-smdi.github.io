Follow a customer enquiry into the work behind it: the next sales action, the active case, the permissions and the payment dates.

By [Ahmadreza Samadi](https://a-samadi.com/about.html)

## Give each system a clear job

Co-Founder & CEO, Royal Abraj Group, Dubai. The group covers property brokerage and documents-clearing services; he leads its digital strategy and technology.

The readings here focus on those everyday product and operating decisions. Start with the actual customer journey, decide where technology helps, define access before connecting an assistant, and keep payment timing visible.
