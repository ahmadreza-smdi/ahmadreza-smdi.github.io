Building business systems in Dubai

Follow a customer enquiry into the work behind it: the next sales action, the active case, the permissions and the payment dates.

By Ahmadreza Samadi

Give each system a clear job

At Royal Abraj Group in Dubai, my work as Co-Founder & CEO connects websites, CRM workflows, customer tools and internal operations. A website explains a service and captures an enquiry; the CRM supports follow-up; customer and operational tools support the work being delivered.

The readings here focus on those everyday product and operating decisions. Start with the actual customer journey, decide where technology helps, define access before connecting an assistant, and keep payment timing visible.

Read the Royal Abraj case study: See why a lead’s next sales action and a case’s next delivery action need distinct responsibilities.

Keep these questions in view

  • What happens after an enquiry, and who owns the next step?
  • What evidence and permissions does the system need?
  • When does cash arrive, what is due first, and what covers the gap?
