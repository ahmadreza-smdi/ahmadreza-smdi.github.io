## General workflow concepts

A data workflow can distinguish data arrival, transformation and downstream use. These stages provide a way to discuss preparation and analytical needs.

## Separating the stages of data preparation

Bronze, Silver and Gold are labels for stages of data preparation. As a general illustration, they can separate ingestion, transformation and downstream use; this is not a description of the Oxfam Novib implementation.

Questions about sensitive data, retention and operating documentation belong in planning a data workflow. Any specific implementation needs its own requirements and review.
