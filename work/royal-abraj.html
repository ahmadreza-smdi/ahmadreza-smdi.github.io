Connecting the customer journey

A customer enquiry is the beginning of a workflow. The business still needs to understand the request, follow up, manage the work and keep the customer informed.

As Co-Founder & CEO of Royal Abraj Group, I lead digital strategy and technology across a Dubai-based business spanning property brokerage and documents-clearing services. My work covers websites, CRM workflows, customer tools and internal operations.

The central design decision is to give each system a clear purpose. The website explains the service and captures the enquiry. The CRM supports sales follow-up. Customer and operational tools support case information, documents, responsibilities and delivery status.

These boundaries matter because a sales record and an active customer case answer different questions. Someone following up on an enquiry needs the next sales action. Someone delivering a service needs the current work, the responsible person and the information required to proceed.

My contribution is to connect those needs through the product and technical decisions. It brings technology leadership into the everyday work of running a business.

My work at Royal Abraj complements my role as Co-Founder & Technical Lead at Appraiva, where I focus on AI real estate intelligence and product architecture.
