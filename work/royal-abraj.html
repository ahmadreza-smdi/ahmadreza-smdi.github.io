Founder case study

Ahmadreza Samadi at Royal Abraj Group

Connecting digital strategy with customer experience and business operations.

By Ahmadreza Samadi · Co-Founder & CEO · 2022 to present · Dubai, UAE

My role

I co-founded Royal Abraj Group, a Dubai-based business spanning property brokerage and documents-clearing services. As Co-Founder & CEO, I lead digital strategy and technology across its websites, customer relationship management workflows, customer tools and internal operations.

My contribution brings together the commercial and technical sides of the business: understanding how a customer enters the process, how a team follows up, and what information each person needs to move the work forward. The aim is to make the technology useful in daily operations.

The business problem

A customer journey can begin with a website enquiry and continue through sales conversations, document requests and ongoing case updates. Each stage has different requirements. A contact record alone does not explain the status of a customer’s request, and an internal message is not a reliable substitute for a clear operational record.

My work addresses those handoffs by connecting the public website experience with structured follow-up and dedicated customer and employee tools.

The systems I lead

  • Websites and enquiry capture. Clear service information and enquiry routes help the team understand the customer’s request before following up.
  • CRM and sales follow-up. Structured lead records support the pre-customer journey, including enquiries and the next action for the team.
  • Customer tools. A dedicated customer experience supports access to case information and document-related workflows.
  • Internal operations. Employee tools organize customer work, responsibilities and delivery status so the team can act on a shared record.

How I approach the technology

I keep the purpose of each system clear. Public information belongs on the website. Sales follow-up belongs in the CRM. Customer cases and documents belong in the appropriate customer and operational systems. Those distinctions shape the product design, access boundaries and handoffs between teams.

Royal Abraj Group is the public umbrella brand. The business operates across separate property-brokerage and documents-clearing entities, so the digital experience also needs to make the relevant service and responsible business clear.

What this says about my work

My role at Royal Abraj connects technology leadership with the practical work of running a business. It complements my work as Co-Founder & Technical Lead at Appraiva, where I focus on AI real estate intelligence and product architecture. Across both roles, I work on the connection between a business problem, a useful product and the systems needed to operate it.
