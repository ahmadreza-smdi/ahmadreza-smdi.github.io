Hamrah e Aval (MCI)

Machine Learning Engineer · November 2020–April 2022 · Tehran

By Ahmadreza Samadi

Ahmadreza Samadi worked as a Machine Learning Engineer at Hamrah e Aval (MCI) in Tehran from November 2020 to April 2022. His work focused on customer segmentation models for database marketing, using RFM analysis and behavioral data.

Decision: support customer segmentation

The work connected customer data with segmentation for database marketing. Samadi’s responsibilities included model development, churn-risk analysis and campaign targeting.

Approach: connect RFM and behavioral data

RFM refers to recency, frequency and monetary analysis. Samadi used RFM together with behavioral data in customer segmentation models. The segmentation work provided a basis for analyzing different customer groups.

Model development and use

Samadi developed and tuned machine learning models for segmentation and memory use. The work supported churn-risk analysis and campaign targeting within the database-marketing context.
