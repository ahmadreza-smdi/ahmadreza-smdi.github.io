Appraiva: turning property imagery into decision support

Building the technical foundation for AI-driven real estate intelligence.

By Ahmadreza Samadi · Co-Founder & Technical Lead · October 2023–present

From property imagery to a useful decision

An image can show the condition of a property. Turning that observation into useful investment research takes more than an AI model.

At Appraiva, a US-based company in Cleveland, Ohio, I work remotely. My role as Co-Founder & Technical Lead connects that analysis with the rest of the product: property data, enrichment, search and the workflows customers use to assess opportunities. I lead the technical direction across product architecture, data systems, AI workflows and cloud infrastructure, alongside customer discovery and go-to-market work.

The central product decision is to treat imagery analysis as part of a research workflow. A condition signal needs context: which property it describes, what other information is available, and how a customer can find and use it. Those questions connect model output with product design and engineering.

Appraiva's January 2024 launch announcement reported that early customers were using the beta platform to find residential investment opportunities. It identified my founding role under the title used at the time, Co-Founder & Lead Data Architect.

This is the kind of work I want to be known for: connecting a technical capability with a useful customer decision.

How the pieces fit together

My technical work connects four parts of the product:

  • Property discovery: connect imagery analysis to the property it describes.
  • Data enrichment: bring condition signals together with other property information.
  • Search: make that information available within an investor's research workflow.
  • Product and infrastructure: connect the interface, data systems, AI workflows and cloud services that support those steps.

The architectural challenge is keeping those connections useful: an image observation needs a property identity, supporting context and a clear next step in the product.
