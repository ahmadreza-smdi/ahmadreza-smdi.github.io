What makes useful evidence

RICS explains why transaction timing, relevant property differences and evidence quality matter. Its first-edition guidance cautions that asking prices may differ from agreed prices and that adjustments require judgment. Historical evidence can still be useful in context. RICS: Comparable evidence in real estate valuation (1st edition, October 2019; opened 28 October 2026)

Evidence, match, gaps

Our original three checks are evidence → match → gaps. Evidence: what happened, when, and how was it verified? Match: compare location, size, condition and terms. Gaps: record what is missing and what needs professional adjustment. Do not invent a fixed uplift for a feature.

Keep uncertainty visible

For the limits of an automated estimate, read AI property valuation: what an estimate misses.

A nearby listing can help frame questions; it cannot alone prove value. Keep uncertainty visible and request the evidence behind an estimate rather than selecting the closest map pin.
