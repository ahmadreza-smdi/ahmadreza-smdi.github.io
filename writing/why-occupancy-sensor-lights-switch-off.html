Why did the lights go out while you were there?

A sensor can miss the clue that you are there.

By Ahmadreza Samadi · 15 October 2026

Motion is a clue. Not a head count.

A passive infrared sensor detects changes in infrared energy within its view. It does not count people.

Detection and the timer differ.

The controller may wait after the last detection before switching lights off. A timeout does not prove the room is empty.

The room can hide the movement.

A partition or poor placement can reduce what the sensor sees. Small, seated movements can be harder to detect.
