AI agents: ability is not authority

A fictional invoice explains reading, drafting and approval boundaries in business AI.

Ahmadreza Samadi · 2026-10-21

English film with synthetic narration and English captions. Mina, the workshop, helper and invoice are fictional. Original animation, not product-test footage.

A useful helper, a separate money decision

Mina and her workshop are fictional. Her helper reads an invoice and drafts a reply. A slip inside the attachment asks it to pay a different account. The story separates information to process from authority to act; it is not an account of an actual fraud or product test.

The boundary belongs in the tools

Our read, change, approve card

List each resource the helper may read, each change it may make, and every action requiring separate authorization. For our fictional invoice: read the document, draft the response, route it to review, and keep payment authority separate. Test the boundary before using real records. This is a decision framework, not a guarantee of security.
