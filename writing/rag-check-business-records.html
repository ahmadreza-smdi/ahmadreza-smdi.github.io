The confident answer from the old price list

How retrieval helps

Retrieval-augmented generation, or RAG, supplies external material to a model before it drafts an answer. Microsoft describes retrieval, added context and generation. Anthropic explains how isolated document chunks can lose context. Retrieval can help; it does not guarantee a correct answer. Microsoft: Retrieval augmented generation (RAG) and indexes in Microsoft Foundry (opened 28 October 2026) Anthropic: Introducing Contextual Retrieval (19 September 2024; opened 28 October 2026)

Record, date, answer

Our practical check is record → date → answer. Record: can you open the exact supporting passage? Date: is this the currently approved version, rather than merely a recently retrieved file? Answer: does that passage actually support this quote and its conditions?

When the evidence is missing

For the fictional hotel, a useful response names the approved sheet and relevant conditions. If that record is missing, pause the quote and say what is missing. Do not turn a search result into invented certainty.

Limits

This framework is an editorial decision aid, not a benchmark or a security guarantee. A citation is a route to evidence, not proof of every generated sentence. This machine-assisted article has source checks; it does not claim human fact-checking.
