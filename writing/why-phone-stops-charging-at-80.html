Why does your phone stop charging at 80%?

A chosen limit, a learned routine and a heat pause can look similar. They have different triggers and different ways to resume charging.

By Ahmadreza Samadi · 5 October 2026

Watch the film

67-second animated film with English narration and subtitles. The phone, settings and battery-wear chart are illustrations, not measured data. Features vary by model and software. Narration is AI-generated.

1. A limit you chose

Some phones stop near a chosen ceiling. Supported iPhones offer limits from 80% to 100%; supported Pixels offer 80%. An occasional full charge can keep the battery estimate accurate. Apple and Google describe the settings.

2. A routine the phone learned

Optimised charging can delay filling the battery so it reaches full near your usual unplugging time. This differs from a fixed limit: it is intended to finish at 100%. It may not engage on every night. Google explains Adaptive Charging.

3. A battery that is too warm

An iPhone may limit charging above 80% when temperatures are too high, then resume after cooling. Changing a chosen limit is not a fix for heat. Let the phone cool in a suitable environment. Apple temperature guidance and charging support.

Less time full, less heat

Apple says reducing time spent fully charged helps reduce wear. Heat can also shorten battery life. Check the battery message and settings to understand the cause before changing anything. There is no promised lifespan gain here, and the film’s wear chart is illustrative.
