Why does AI need weather stations?

A clever model still needs clues from the real world.

By Ahmadreza Samadi · 15 October 2026

The missing clue might be nearby.

A distant station cannot describe every local shower. Ground observations add local evidence.

A radar picture is not a prediction.

Radar shows detected precipitation. A forecast estimates what may happen next. Keep the time labels separate.

Before trusting the rain icon...

Check the location, update time and forecast window. Use official local warnings for safety decisions. Our original three-question reading framework uses fictional scenes, not measured results. Research funding is not completed deployment or proof of accuracy. Illustrative maps and weather only; no local forecast or safety advice beyond official warnings.
