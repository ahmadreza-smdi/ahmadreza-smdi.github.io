How project escrow works

DLD describes an escrow account in a project’s name, holding off-plan payments. Its FAQ explains construction-stage checks before trustee-authorised releases. The activation service also has approval requirements. This is controlled disbursement, not a developer’s unrestricted general pot. [DLD] [DLDACT]

Match, verify, track

Our original three-check framework is match → verify → track. Match the project identity across the sales documents and official record. Verify account details through official channels before acting on payment instructions. Track the official project status and record when you checked it.

Two different payment questions

Keep two questions separate: when a buyer owes an instalment, and when a trustee can release project funds. Do not infer one automatically from the other. Read the actual contract and applicable requirements.

Limits

Escrow alone does not establish on-time handover, investment returns or an automatic refund. This general explanation is not legal or investment advice and does not resolve a particular transaction.
