A beautiful building model can still be out of date

In a fictional office, Omar sees a polished building model while a room feels wrong. The useful question is not how realistic the model looks. It is which physical asset the data describes and when that data was observed.

A practical framework

Our original check is asset → feed → freshness → verification. Match the record to the right equipment. Identify the data feed. Display its observation time and update condition. If it becomes stale, show the gap rather than continue presenting it as current. Ask who verifies a decision against the building.
